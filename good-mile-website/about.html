About

Brokerage built on carrier experience

Good Mile is a freight brokerage built by people with more than 20 years of experience on the carrier side of trucking.

Photo: trucks at a distribution centre in early morning light
Our story

We learned this business from the road up

Before Good Mile, our team spent more than two decades on the carrier side: dispatching trucks, working with drivers, meeting appointments and solving problems at the dock.

Good Mile brings that operating knowledge to brokerage. We know what a shipper needs to hear and when, and we know what a carrier needs to do the job well.

What that means for you

What we bring to every load

Real trucking background

Decisions are made by people who have managed freight from the carrier’s side.

Respect for drivers

We understand driver hours, dock time and what makes a load run smoothly.

Appointments that hold

We plan around pickup and delivery windows, and tell you early if anything changes.

Reefer and dry van depth

Our strongest experience and relationships are in these two modes.

Modern visibility

Tracking, milestones and documents are available to your team online.
