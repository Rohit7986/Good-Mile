Freight security

How we vet the carriers that move your freight

Good Mile reviews carrier authority, insurance, safety information, identity, fraud indicators, and compliance before tendering customer freight.

Our standards

Ten checks behind every carrier

Screening standards are applied alongside shipment visibility and ongoing operational monitoring.

01

Operating authority verification

We confirm the carrier holds the operating authority required for the load.

02

Insurance verification

We verify that required coverage is valid and appropriate before freight is tendered.

03

Safety and compliance review

Safety and compliance information is reviewed as part of carrier approval.

04

Carrier identity verification

We confirm we are dealing with the carrier itself, not someone using its name.

05

Fraud indicator review

Carriers are checked for known signs of freight fraud.

06

Double-brokering risk review

We look for signs that a load may be re-brokered without authorization.

07

Contact and domain consistency

Phone numbers, email domains and contacts are checked against the carrier’s records.

08

Shipment tracking

Tracking is required where practical, and exceptions are documented.

09

Ongoing re-evaluation

Carriers are reviewed again when material information about them changes.

10

Higher scrutiny for higher risk

High-value and high-risk freight receives additional review where appropriate.

Straight talk

What screening can and cannot do

Careful vetting lowers risk. No screening process can remove every possibility of theft, delay, fraud or loss, and we will not tell you otherwise.

Sources we rely on

  • Federal FMCSA records for authority, safety and insurance filings
  • Direct verification of insurance coverage
  • Carrier identity and contact checks
  • Shipment tracking and documented exceptions
